Welcome! I am a Ph.D. candidate in Economics at [National University of Singapore](https://www.nus.edu.sg/) (NUS). I am currently a visiting PhD student at [Center for Law and Economics](https://lawecon.ethz.ch/), ETH Zurich (Sept. 2026 – Jan. 2027)

My research lies at the intersection of behavioral and experimental economics and artificial intelligence. I study large language models (LLMs) from two perspectives: as agents that inherit human-like behavioral biases, and as information providers whose outputs shape human decision-making. My work combines online and field experiments to better understand human--AI interaction and its implications for social welfare.

For more information, please see my [curriculum vitae]({{ site.github.url }}/assets/cv.pdf).
